Mural festivals in Rotterdam

The flagship festival of Rotterdam is “ALL CAPS” (formerly “POW! WOW! Rotterdam.”) Its former role was being the Dutch version of the international street art festival. But now, it has transitioned into an independent, locally rooted event. The festival turns urban neighborhoods into outdoor galleries with no entrance fee or closing times. It has large-scale murals created by national and international street artists. The very first European edition launched in the Merwe-Vierhavengebied area in 2018. It later moved to Rotterdam-South in the Feijenoord and Afrikaanderwijk neighborhoods. The organization split from the global “POW! WOW! / Worldwide Walls” and rebranded as “ALL CAPS” to chart its own course and focus on local community impact.
